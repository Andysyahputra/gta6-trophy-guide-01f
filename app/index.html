Roadmap to the platinum

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story. Chapters 1–4 and the final mission, plus the mission trophies along the way. Do the Chapter 1 meditation spot early.
  2. Stage 2 — Strangers & side missions. Finish the strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and 8 stranger strands across Leonida.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities & skills. 250 stunts, gold medals at the Shooting Range, races, fishing, challenges and combat feats.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & cleanup. Card sets, WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles, all 52 areas, garage properties, then 100% completion and the platinum.
